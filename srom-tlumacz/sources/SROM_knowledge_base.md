## Publisher

- **Current (from vol. 18 / 2025):** Komitet Opieki nad Zabytkami Kultury Żydowskiej w Tarnowie / The Committee for the Preservation of Jewish Cultural Heritage in Tarnów.
- **Past (through vol. 17 / 2024):** Muzeum Ziemi Tarnowskiej / Tarnów Region Museum (formerly: Muzeum Okręgowe w Tarnowie / District Museum in Tarnów).
- **Origin story:** The journal grew out of work at the Ethnographic Museum in Tarnów (mentioned in the LinkedIn About page as the institution that pioneered systematic collection of Romani artefacts in the 1990s and hosted the permanent Roma history exhibition). This is historical context/provenance, not the current publisher. Use only in origin-story framing, not in masthead/application metadata.

## Peer review status

**Current:** The journal is **peer-reviewed**. A minimal but genuine double-blind peer review system is implemented for Parts I–II (scholarly articles). Part III, *Recenzje – Informacje – Opinie* (reviews, information, opinion pieces), is not peer-reviewed.

**Supporting infrastructure improvements:** Alongside peer review, infrastructure is being built to meet gateway-database requirements:
- Website rebuild underway (to hold DOI-linked digital versions of articles)
- DOI registration recently initiated (starting with most recent issue)
- Highwire `citation_*` meta tags to be implemented for Google Scholar / CrossRef indexing
- Implementation plan for ERIH+/DOAJ compliance being finalized

## Indexing and ranking

- **Currently indexed in:** CEJSH, CEEOL.
- **Target gateway databases for MNiSW re-qualification:** ERIH+, DOAJ, EBSCO-Ultimate, ICI JML. Journal is not currently in any of these four (which are the gatekeepers for the new MNiSW/MEiN points system).
- **MNiSW/MEiN points:** held 20 points (July 2023); currently zero (delisted when the new points regulation took effect).
- **Timeline for listing:** goal is re-listing within one year. This drives prioritization of ERIH+ application and website/DOI infrastructure completion.

## Format and cadence

- Annual, one issue per year, typically published in December.
- Non-fiction/scholarly + a non-reviewed section (*Recenzje – Informacje – Opinie*, Part III).
