   account: shipped unchanged as general-purpose skills. `unlazy` is in the account (anthropic-skills:unlazy): named
   as a dependency, not shipped.
7. Not shipped: srom-checkup, `_handoffs/` history, `_widok/`, `mb_view.py`, hooks, gates/plans of finished leaves,
   `dump/`, `dist/`, `indesign/legacy/v2/`, `tools/indesign_check/` (tool building stays in Code), derived `build/`
