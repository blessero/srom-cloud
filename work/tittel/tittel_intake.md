## 4. Doubts in the source (flagged, not corrected; what I would do)

1. **"linguistic studies in the 1870s/-80s"** (§ 2, before n. 35): the author has just said (§ 2) that the language-based
   theory "gained popularity" in the 1780s (Büttner 1771, Rüdiger 1782, Grellmann 1783). "1870s" is almost certainly a
   slip for "1770s". Translated as written („w latach 70. i 80. XIX wieku”); query to the author; I would correct to
   „XVIII wieku” once she confirms.
2. **n. 49: "s. 743"** — the quoted passage ("two sorts of people") is on MEW 23, **p. 741** (online MEW text with its
   pagination; the other nine MEW pages check out). Token unchanged; query (information: an obvious page slip, § 12.2.8).
3. "Berliner Monatsschrift" in the text vs *Berlinische Monatsschrift* in n. 18 (srom-typeset D20 B5): kept as written;
   srom-typeset proposes the correct title in the translation — I would follow it once MB approves B.
4. "a meeting of the princes and rulers of the Swabian district within Wurttemberg" — the Swabian Circle was not within
   Württemberg (it covered many territories; its diet met in Ulm); probably "including Württemberg". Translated as
   written („okręgu szwabskiego w Wirtembergii”); query to the author. I would write „okręgu szwabskiego, do którego
   należała Wirtembergia” once she confirms.
5. "defended by German courts until 1962" — the usual dating of the Federal Court's change of view is its ruling of
   18.12.1963 (literature; not checked in two sources here). As written; query to the author.
6. "the English term “gypsy”" (n. 35 context: "a theory that manifested in the English term") — fine; no action.
7. Kant quotation n. 44: the author's "be farmers [*ansäßige Landanbauer*] or manual laborers" simplifies Kant's "ein zu
   ansässigen Landanbauern oder Handarbeitern tauglicher Schlag" (a stock fit to be…). Translated as the author quotes;
   no action.
8. Marx n. 71/75: "charm of the gipsy life" is the English translation's, not Marx's German (see § 1): translator's note.
9. Headings 4 and 5 open with a closing quotation mark (B10) and "these ’findings’": typographic, Polish marks set.
10. n. 13: "Kleingeld's defense of Kant" — Pauline Kleingeld (feminine forms in Polish: „obrona Kanta przez Kleingeld”).
