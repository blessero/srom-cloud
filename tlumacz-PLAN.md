# Plan: srom-tlumacz v1 — EN→PL translation module for Studia Romologica

Depth: tree 4   Mode: orchestrated (one leaf per conversation in the SROM_Naczelny Project; no subagents in claude.ai)
Budget note: several sessions. Leaf 1.2 (baseline) precedes the asset leaves by design: remedies are built for measured error classes, not imagined ones.

Scope decisions (MB, 24.09.2026): EN→PL only; no PL→EN component — one exception (MB, 25.09.2026): author keywords of Polish-original articles are translated PL→EN for metadata, using the same termbase. Vol. 18 terminology is binding house precedent. Tie-break order for competing Polish equivalents: established Polish usage > SROM consistency > fidelity to source concept > clarity for non-specialists (encoded with a fidelity floor, see tlumacz-tb-schema.md).

### Per-article outputs (IDs = master CSV `article_id`)

srom-tlumacz is the translation step inside srom-produkcja's scenario C. The contract's source of truth is srom-produkcja `references/handoff.md`; `tlumacz-test_handoff.py` proves the parts relied on here. In: `<id>_src.md` (frozen SROM-MD, every reference a `[@key …]` token) + `refs.json`. Out: the files below. The Word working copy `<id>_robocza.docx` in `work/<id>/` is the master from the editor's first edit (never exported over); the delivered `<id>_pl.md` is its import ("Back", T26). srom-tlumacz does not duplicate what srom-produkcja enforces: structure, note markers, citation keys, numbers, typography, kanon lint, DOCX.

- OUT-TEXT `<id>_pl.md` — SROM-MD (srom-produkcja `references/srom-md.md`) translated from `<id>_src.md`, paragraph for paragraph; tokens, markers, locators and block lines copied unchanged; translator notes `[^t<n>]` ending `– przyp. tłum.` (printed as a separate asterisk series, MB 25.09.2026; rendering is E8); added citations declared by `"srom-added"` on their entry in `<id>_refs_tlum.json` (primary: survives Word edits; the in-note `<!-- DODANO: @key -->` still works); translator(s) in YAML front matter `tlumaczenie:` at the top (T7; not printed, reported by the build as `translators_struct`); translation note in `::: przypis-tytulowy`, one block per article, the author's own title note as a further paragraph (D12, Kanon v1.7 § 7.1). In `::: przyklad` line 1 (form) is unchanged, line 2 gets Polish lexical glosses with Leipzig category labels unchanged, line 3 is translated in ‘ ’. Must give `check.py --pair <id>_src.md <id>_pl.md --refs …` → `CHECK OK`. Anything unresolved stays in the text as a comment: `<!-- PRZYWRÓCIĆ ORYGINAŁ: … -->` for § 12.2.4 a–b, `<!-- DO SPRAWDZENIA: … -->` for OPEN terms and author queries. **Comments never block** (`handoff.md`: "Nothing in comments blocks anything"; the build strips them with a warning and still reports PASS). In the Word working copy they become Word comments; after import they leave the text and are listed only in the import report. So every open comment is also a line in the article's notes sheet (`work/<id>/<id>_uwagi.md`), open until MB closes it; neither the pair check nor the build will catch it.
- OUT-REVIEW — not produced so far (29.09.2026 17:46): MB reviews the Word working copy, and the notes sheet `<id>_uwagi.md` (numbered S-items: pages and wordings to look up, choices, doubts) carries what the review sheet was meant to; 1.4.3 decides whether the sheet below is still wanted. Planned: `<id>_review.docx` — bilingual review sheet built from `src.md` and `pl.md` (1:1 paragraph alignment is guaranteed by the pair check): one row per paragraph and per note; columns: ref, EN, PL, flags (TB, QUOTE, QUERY, NUM, NAME). Format fixed in 1.4.3.
- OUT-QUERIES `<id>_pytania_tlum.csv` — UTF-8 with BOM, `;`, header `adresat;rodzaj;przypis;dzieło;szczegóły`; merged by `build.py --queries` into the one article sheet. `adresat`: autor / redakcja. `rodzaj` in Polish, like the build's own rows: „błąd w oryginale — do decyzji”, „termin do rozstrzygnięcia”, „cytat — wydanie polskie / oryginał do ustalenia”, „niejednoznaczność oryginału — pytanie do autora”. `przypis`: the note label from `<id>_src.md` (stable; printed numbers are not known before the Word round trip, and `[^t…]` labels do not survive it — see E6); location of body-text items in `szczegóły`. Errors in the source are recorded, never silently corrected.
- OUT-QUOTES `<id>_quotes.tsv` — quotation re-sourcing sheet; columns: ID, location, cited work, class (PL-EDITION, PL-ORIGINAL, THIRD-LANG, EN-NO-PL, VERSE), action, status. Classes map one-to-one to kanon draft § 12.2.4 a–e; every open row has exactly one comment in `pl.md` and one open line in the notes sheet (comments don't block; see OUT-TEXT). Status values: `done`; `open (S<n>)` naming that notes-sheet item; `optional` for an improvement that is not a decision for MB (1.4.2a, 29.09.2026 14:18). Checked by `tlumacz-draft_check.py <id> --quotes`.
- OUT-FRONT `<id>_front_pl.md` — Polish title, subtitle, abstract, keywords (Kanon § 12.2.2; T11). Format: docstring of `tlumacz-front_check.py`; `tlumacz-front_check.py <id>_src_front.md <id>_front_pl.md` → `FRONT OK`. Header data for the master CSV; the build does not read it (T13).
- OUT-TBROWS — no separate file so far: new rows go straight into `tlumacz-tb.tsv` as PROVISIONAL (1.3.2) and become HOUSE on MB's sign-off (§ Files and ownership). Planned `<id>_tb-rows.tsv` only if a text needs rows kept apart before sign-off. The per-article glossary used while translating is generated from the termbase by `tb_lookup` — a derived view, never edited by hand.
- OUT-DELIVERY — after each editing round of the editor (`handoff.md` "Back", T26, 29.09.2026): first (T30, 01.10.2026) rename the editor's master to `<id>_robocza.docx` and older exports to `<id>_robocza_old<n>.docx` (a `_v2` master is common; `take_back.py` takes only that name; tested by a case in `tlumacz-test_handoff.py`); then `docx_in.py <id>_robocza.docx -o <id>_pl.md` in `work/<id>/` (the md is never edited by hand afterwards), then `check.py --pair`, `build.py … --pair-src --queries --draft`, `tlumacz-front_check.py`; then an E-item `## E<n> — [<Author>] delivery: …` with the sha256 of `<id>_robocza.docx`, `<id>_pl.md`, `<id>_front_pl.md` and, when they exist, `<id>_refs_tlum.json`, `<id>_pytania_tlum.csv`; also the sha256 of srom-produkcja's `refs.json` checked against and the verdicts. A new round = a new item that supersedes the old. srom-produkcja takes it with `take_back.py` (sha256, md = fresh import of the master, pair check) and builds from its copy.
- OUT-REFS `<id>_refs_tlum.json` — CSL-JSON entries the translation adds (Polish editions, Polish originals: Kanon § 12.2.4 a–b), per srom-md.md conventions. Every entry carries `"srom-added": "tlum"` and a non-empty `"srom-source"` (library-catalogue record URL or verified ISBN): data taken from a catalogue record, never from memory; a missing field stays missing (`[BRAK …]`). Verified by `tlumacz-test_handoff.py` (26.09.2026), also after the Word round trip.

### Conventions

Termbase: TSV, UTF-8, tab-separated, one header row, multi-values separated by ` | `. Dates in kanon format `dd.mm.rrrr`. Precedent quotes («…») are the anchors; line numbers refer to `sources/Studia_Romologica_nr_18_2025.txt` (text extraction, CRLF stripped; `md <art>` anchors point to `sources/vol18-md/`) and are informative only — the checker searches the whole whitespace-normalised text and reports a moved line as a warning. Once the final DOCX of vol. 18 arrives, anchors are re-pointed to it in 1.3.1.
